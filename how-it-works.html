How it works

From checkout to a running strategy

The AutoTrade website exists to explain the product, take payment, and get the app onto your machine. Everything after that happens inside the Windows application.

01

Choose your plan

Select the AutoTrade subscription — Essential, Pro, or Unlimited — that fits the markets and volume you trade.

02

Download AutoTrade

Install the Windows application from the download page after your subscription is active.

03

Connect your broker

Open AutoTrade and connect a supported brokerage account through the application.

04

Configure & run

Choose a preset strategy or customize one, set your risk controls, and monitor activity from AutoTrade.

Architecture

Where each part of the system lives

Customer → AutoTrade Website → Stripe Checkout → Windows App Download → AutoTrade Desktop App → Supported Broker API

Trade execution happens only in the highlighted step — the AutoTrade desktop application connected to your broker. The website itself never places trades.

Good to know

What the website does vs. what the app does

AutoTrade Website

Explains the product, shows pricing, processes your subscription through Stripe, hosts the Windows installer, and provides account and support information.

AutoTrade Desktop App

Connects to your broker, runs your chosen strategy, monitors markets, executes supported trades, and shows your analytics — all from your own Windows PC.
